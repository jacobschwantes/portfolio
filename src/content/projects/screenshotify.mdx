## What it does

1. **Screenshotify** is a screenshot API that allows you to take screenshots of
   websites at scale without the hassle of browser setup and infrastructure
   maintenance.

## Why I built it

- **Curious about building web services** and wanted to take a stab at creating
  a useful product.
- **I already had users**. I previously shipped a simpler version of the product
  by finding a way to utilize **Puppeteer** within [Vercel](https://vercel.com)
  cloud functions, which have a generous free tier. To my surprise, there were
  people who had discovered it organically and were using it within their
  products.
- **Wanted to take the idea further** by making it more robust and feature
  complete while learning about authentication, interface design, serverless
  architecture, and more.

## What I learned

1. **Authentication is a pain**

I had never made an app where you could sign in before, I took this chance to
learn about Firebase and PaaS (platform as a service) offerings.

2. **Responsive design can be tricky to nail**

How do you design a dashboard that is both easy to use and looks good?
Responsive graphs, dark mode, and more.

3. **Serverless architecture is a double edged sword**

How do you make a service cheap to run, responsive, and scalable? I explored
google cloud, fine tuned cloud run configs for perforfmance and cost efficienty,
learned about dependency caching, Cloudflare, firewalls, and more.

4. **Cloud storage is a game changer**

How do you store and serve hundreds of thousands of images? I learned about
cloud storage buckets and how to serve images in them from a public URL.

5. **Rate limiting is a tricky problem**

How do you prevent abuse? Efficient rate limiting is a tricky problem to solve,
but well worth it.

6. **Using headless browsers is tricky**

making them work reliably is challenging, but rewarding. They're used all over
the industry for testing, web scraping, and more.
